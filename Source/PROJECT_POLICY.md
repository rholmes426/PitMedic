# PitMedic project policy

PitMedic is intended to remain completely free and ad-free.

PitMedic is free and open-source software licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later. Anyone may inspect, use, modify, and redistribute it under that license. Distributed modified versions must preserve the license and corresponding-source obligations.

- No subscriptions, paid tiers, license keys, activations, or feature locks.
- No advertising, affiliate marketing, bundled offers, or sponsored repair recommendations inside the application.
- No sale of user data.
- No payment processing, donor account, or donor identity tracking inside the application.
- Every monitoring, diagnostic, repair, restore, and history feature remains available regardless of whether a user contributes.
- Optional financial support may be accepted through a separate hosted page on pitmedic.com.
- Contributions are voluntary, do not unlock benefits, and do not provide preferential support.
- Public wording should use “Support PitMedic” or “voluntary contribution” and must not imply that contributions are tax-deductible.

Simulator names are used only as compatibility references. PitMedic should not use publisher artwork or imply affiliation or endorsement without written permission.

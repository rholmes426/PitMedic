Update, server, and circuit failures

Immediately after an update

A previously working installation stops launching or loading content. Package integrity, changed configuration, and update timing are the strongest clues.

While joining a server

The simulator reaches the menu but exits during connection or session loading. Track, car, package, network-adjacent, and rendering evidence need to be separated.

When loading a circuit

A repeatable failure on one combination can point toward content or configuration, while failures across every circuit more often shift attention toward the graphics stack or system.

Spend less time troubleshooting

Catch the next failure in context.

PitMedic records the surrounding evidence so an update, content problem, and PC problem do not all look the same.

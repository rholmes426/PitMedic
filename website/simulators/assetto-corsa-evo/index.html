Update, graphics, and session failures leave different evidence behind. Pick the closest point below to focus the investigation.

Early-access builds change over time, so the date an issue began and what the update changed are essential context before older advice is applied.

Graphics

During graphics initialization

An immediate close, black screen, or rendering error makes graphics-driver events, display configuration, overlays, and GPU stability especially relevant.

Driving session

When entering a driving session

If the menus work but the session does not, track, car, configuration, and load-stage evidence become more useful than basic launch troubleshooting.

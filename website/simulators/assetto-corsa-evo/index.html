Where to look

Common problem categories

Because Assetto Corsa EVO continues to evolve, updates can change symptoms and fixes. PitMedic focuses on current evidence and clearly labels what it knows rather than guessing.

  • Graphics-driver or rendering failures
  • Configuration changes after simulator updates
  • Installation, runtime, or permission problems
  • Overlay and background-application conflicts
  • CPU, GPU, memory, or thermal pressure

Frequently asked questions

Assetto Corsa EVO troubleshooting

Why did EVO stop launching after an update?

An update can expose configuration, runtime, graphics-driver, or file-integrity problems. PitMedic records the launch evidence and offers supported next steps.

Can PitMedic separate a game problem from a PC problem?

It can often separate simulator evidence from Windows, driver, and hardware evidence, while showing uncertainty when the cause is not proven.

Will PitMedic automatically change EVO files?

No. A supported repair is explained first, backed up when applicable, and performed only under PitMedic’s safe repair rules.

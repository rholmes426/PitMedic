iRacing crashes, freezes, or won’t launch?

Launch, load, or race

Start with the moment it failed.

Launch, loading, and race failures leave different evidence behind. Pick the closest point below to focus the investigation.

Launch

Before the simulator opens

The launcher hands off to the simulator, but the process closes or never reaches the loading screen. Startup, runtime, permission, and Windows application events matter most here.

Loading

While joining a session

The failure appears after selecting a car or track. Graphics initialization, configuration, content, and session-loading evidence become more useful.

On track

During a race

A sudden exit, freeze, or black screen after several laps points attention toward driver resets, thermal history, memory pressure, and nearby system events.

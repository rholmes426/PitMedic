iRacing

iRacing Helper Service stopped / UI cannot launch

The iRacing UI needs the iRacing.com Helper Service. A stopped service can explain Not In Service, but a temporary service handoff during an update is expected.

Automatic repair availableActiveEvidence reviewed 2026-09-01Guide updated 2026-09-29

Checks and expected results

Before you repair it

  • Finish any active iRacing update and allow its settling period to end.
  • Open Windows Services and check iRacing.com Helper Service; record whether it is missing, stopped, or running.
  • If multiple iRacing copies are installed, compare the service location with the UI shortcut target before restarting anything.

How to confirm the result

After the service restart, confirm its state is Running and retry opening the UI and a test session. A service that immediately stops again needs its error investigated.

Repair limits and when to stop

Restarting the service does not repair a damaged car, UI cache, or mismatched installation. A missing service may require the vendor installation workflow rather than repeated restart attempts.

How PitMedic recognizes it

iRacing.com Helper Service absent or stopped while UI launch fails or reports Not In Service.

Signals PitMedic may recognize

  • Not In Service
  • iRacing.com Helper Service
  • iRacingService

Built-in response

Restart the iRacing service using the installed service/start-stop helpers, then verify service state.

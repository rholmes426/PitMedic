How PitMedic recognizes it

RaceHub app and elevated-helper clean recovery

Controlled recovery sequence

  1. Confirm every supported simulator is closed
  2. Close RaceHub and its known elevated helper
  3. Relaunch the validated RaceHub executable
  4. Verify that RaceHub remains running

Built-in response

RaceRoom Racing Experience

Broken graphics / resolution configuration

PitMedic contains a dedicated diagnostic record for this problem, including the evidence required before it recommends a repair.

Automatic repair availableActiveReviewed 2026-09-01

How PitMedic recognizes it

RaceRoom cannot display correctly, minimizes on launch, shows black output after a GPU/display change, or graphics_options.xml is implicated.

Signals PitMedic may recognize

  • graphics_options.xml
  • resolution
  • refresh rate
  • black screen

Built-in response

Back up and remove UserData\graphics_options.xml so RaceRoom creates a fresh display configuration.

Repair safetyAutomatic / reversible / one-click

Verification sources

PitMedic prioritizes vendor documentation and labels community findings separately.

  • Black screen with AMD GPURaceRoom Steam Community · Community evidence

    A RaceRoom moderator recommends deleting graphics_options.xml after a GPU change when entering a race produces a black screen.

  • Graphic Resolution - settings off screenKW Studios Forum · Community evidence

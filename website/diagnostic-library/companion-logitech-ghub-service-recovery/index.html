Logitech G HUB

Logitech G HUB crash or stale-process recovery

PitMedic contains a dedicated diagnostic record for this problem, including the evidence required before it recommends a repair.

Approval requiredActiveReviewed 2026-09-01
01

How PitMedic recognizes it

Loading-loop, agent-fault, and updater-service recovery

Controlled recovery sequence

  1. Confirm every supported simulator is closed
  2. Close the G HUB UI and agent without deleting settings
  3. Restart the Logitech G HUB updater service
  4. Relaunch the validated G HUB executable
  5. Verify that G HUB remains running
02

Built-in response

PitMedic follows Logitech's loading-loop recovery order: close the G HUB UI and agent, restart the G HUB updater service, relaunch G HUB, and verify that it stays running.

Repair safetyAsk first / requires elevation

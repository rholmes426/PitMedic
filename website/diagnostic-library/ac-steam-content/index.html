Assetto Corsa

Original Assetto Corsa installed game-file verification

PitMedic can start Steam's installed-file verification for the original Assetto Corsa when stock game content needs checking.

Approval requiredActiveEvidence reviewed 2026-11-17Guide updated 2026-11-17

Checks and expected results

Steam checks packaged files for app 244210. Download acceptance is separate from completion, and mods can fail even when the stock installation verifies correctly.

Before you repair it

  • Save any modifications to stock game files separately: Steam may replace those files during verification.
  • Close Assetto Corsa, its launcher and Content Manager, then choose the game-file check in Repair Tools.
  • Approve the estimate, let Steam finish and use a stock car and track for the first retest.

How to confirm the result

Confirm Steam has finished checking/downloading and retry a stock session. If only a modded combination fails, investigate that mod and its compatible CSP/Content Manager version separately.

Repair limits and when to stop

Requires PitMedic 1.0.0.13 or later. This starts supported Steam verification, not a mod/CSP installer, full-profile reset or an automatic driver/runtime change. Steam verification has no PitMedic one-click Restore; preserve modified stock files before starting.

How PitMedic recognizes it

The user selects original Assetto Corsa game-file verification in Repair Tools. A generic mod error or clean launcher exit does not automatically start it.

Built-in response

With approval, start Steam validation for app 244210 after closing AC, its launcher and Content Manager. Steam checks packaged stock files. Wait for downloads and test a stock car and track. Steam may replace modified stock files; preserve those separately first. Third-party mods, CSP and Content Manager are not reinstalled or repaired.
